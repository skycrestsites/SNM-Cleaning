About This Service

Your workplace is a reflection of your business. A clean, organized environment boosts employee morale, creates a professional impression for clients, and contributes to a healthier workspace. SNM Cleaning LLC delivers reliable commercial cleaning services tailored to your schedule and standards.

We service offices, retail stores, medical offices, showrooms, and other commercial spaces throughout New Albany and the greater Columbus area. Whether you need daily janitorial services or a monthly deep clean, we build a customized plan around your needs.
