Post-Construction
Cleaning

Detailed cleaning after construction or renovation projects in New Albany, Ohio. We transform dusty job sites into sparkling, move-in ready spaces, removing dust, debris, stickers, and all construction residue.

About This Service

Renovations and new builds leave behind a unique type of mess that standard cleaning can't handle. Fine drywall dust settles everywhere, in vents, on fixtures, inside cabinets, and debris accumulates in every corner. Our post-construction cleaning service addresses all of it.

We specialize in removing construction residue, sticker adhesive from windows and fixtures, paint overspray, and fine particulate dust from every surface. The result: a fully clean, safe, and occupancy-ready space that your clients, tenants, or family can move right into.
